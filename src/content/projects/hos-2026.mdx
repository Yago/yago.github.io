More than ten years after the first site, Presence Switzerland asked us to rebuild House of Switzerland. The new Drupal site lists Swiss events abroad, the ones already done and the ones still to come. A large event can also have its own short-lived website, linked to the main one. I worked on the frontend. The [case study](https://antistatique.net/en/projets/a-new-platform-for-house-of-switzerland) has the longer version.
