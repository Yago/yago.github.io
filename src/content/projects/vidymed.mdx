Vidymed runs emergency medical centres in Vaud. People often open the site in a hurry, so we kept it simple. The three centres are on the homepage, and you can pick one by age and by the kind of emergency. I worked on the Drupal frontend. More in the [case study](https://antistatique.net/projets/nouveau-site-internet-pour-le-groupe-vidymed).
