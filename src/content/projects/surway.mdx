In eight days, we ran a second internal experiment with [Pierre Georges](https://www.pierregeorges.ch/). This time we made Surway, a small game you play in the browser. Once you finish the first level, what other players did is still there. We built it with TypeScript, React and Phaser, and it works on a phone too. The [blog post](https://antistatique.net/en/blog/experiment-2-surway-a-social-experiments-game) tells how it went.
