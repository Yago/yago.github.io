In just 6 days, we conducted an internal experiment in collaboration with [Pierre Georges](https://www.pierregeorges.ch/). Our goal? To create an AI-powered guided tour of Switzerland, led by Maya the marmot, available in your native language. At each point of interest, you can engage with the AI, asking for more details about the location. This project combines Next.js, Mapbox, and the OpenAI API to deliver a unique experience. Dive into the details in the [blog post](https://antistatique.net/en/blog/experiment-1-switzerland-grand-tour).
